# WoWaS Dialogue and Play Insertion Patch v10

## Purpose
This patch upgrades canon-to-manuscript expansion so dossier notes, side notes, relational notes, and support-cast notes become actual manuscript-bearing dialogue and play scenes rather than remaining implied metadata.

This patch works with:
- `patches/PROSE_AND_TONE_GUIDE.json`

## Core law
The cast must not only survive the world. They must also speak, tease, eat, rest, joke, argue, comfort, flirt, wonder, and enjoy beauty whenever they can.

One of the book's core communications is:
no matter how lost the world may seem, there is hope as long as joy can still be found.

## Manuscript rule
Any note that implies:
- a personal question
- a confession
- a correction
- a tease
- a comfort exchange
- a domestic act
- a moment of play
- a sensory first
- a moral disagreement
- a fascination response
- a relationship-defining memory
must be treated as potential manuscript dialogue, not just support metadata.

## Expansion priority order
When expanding a chapter from canon:
1. preserve the beat
2. insert the strongest relevant dialogue moment
3. insert the strongest relevant lived-world / play moment
4. insert aftermath or emotional carryover
5. only then expand connective narration

## Mandatory recurring scene classes

### 1. Relational truth scenes
Dialogue where a bond changes because someone says what is actually true.

Use especially for:
- Pip / Xethrolund separation guilt and the wrongness of distance
- Pip / Rylos concealment, pressure, and manipulation
- Pip / Rolzen equal investment, interdependence, and presence

### 2. Curiosity scenes
Dialogue driven by fascination with the small things that make someone themselves.

Use especially for:
- Rolzen asking Pip the kinds of questions Pip usually asks others
- Pip learning what returned pieces of Rolzen's self reveal about him
- Pip being startled by being seen so deeply and so gently

### 3. Domestic recovery scenes
Food, sleep, washing, warmth, first comfort, resting together, gear tending, body tending, and quiet companionship.

Use especially for:
- Rolzen's first meaningful magic in nearly a century being used to feed Pip
- Pip waking to new smells, new food, and unfamiliar human domestic tenderness
- post-battle or post-flight grounding

### 4. Play scenes
Teasing, joking, chasing delight, wonder-tour moments, flirtation, dogs settling in, absurdity, trying strange food, marveling at beauty, and letting the party breathe.

These are not filler. They are structural proof that joy is still alive.

### 5. Moral-pressure scenes
Dialogue where Pip judges without condemnation, or where others force him to say what he already knows.

Use especially when:
- someone wants him to soften the truth
- someone mistakes mercy for uncertainty
- someone cannot see what is obvious to him
- someone needs to hear the line named clearly

### 6. Sensory-first scenes
Moments built around first smells, first textures, first tastes, first sights, first body-logic responses, and strange new human habits.

Use especially for:
- newly human Pip
- food selection still shaped by chipmunk logic
- post-portal re-embodiment
- transformation and recovery sequences

## Character-specific dialogue laws

### Pip
- usually speaks naturally, specifically, and honestly
- does not sloganize his worldview
- can be playful, hungry, awkward, blunt, embarrassed, gentle, or piercingly clear
- often reacts to the truth under speech rather than to the speech itself

### Rolzen
- questions should feel hungry, fascinated, warm, specific, and personality-focused
- his care often shows before his flirtation
- even badly damaged, his instinct is still to comfort and feed

### Xethrolund
- dialogue should reveal love of Pip's mind, clarity, adaptability, and perception
- emotional restraint or overcorrection should hurt because the love is real

### Rylos
- charm, teaching warmth, and emotional intelligence must remain visible
- manipulation must arise through concealment and steering, not cartoon villain speech

### Kyreal
- needs real best-friend energy, pressure-release banter, and world-enjoyment moments
- should make the school and class life feel inhabited

## Selection rule for dossier notes
When scanning dossiers or side notes, prioritize insertion of notes that do one or more of the following:
- reveal who a person is in a way action alone cannot
- make love, trust, guilt, or interdependence legible
- make the world feel livable instead of only survivable
- create emotional contrast before devastation
- prove why later loss hurts
- give Pip a chance to receive the kind of attention he usually gives others

## Anti-failure rules
- Do not reduce play scenes to generic downtime.
- Do not reduce dialogue moments to exposition delivery.
- Do not make everyone sound equally eloquent.
- Do not let joy scenes feel tonally disconnected from the danger.
- Do not let suffering erase humor, appetite, fascination, curiosity, or beauty.
- Do not treat relational notes as optional fluff; many of them are final-manuscript load-bearing content.

## Outcome rule
A finished manuscript pass should feel like:
- the canon remained intact
- the characters really lived inside it
- the party found reasons to keep loving the world
- dialogue and play made the hope believable
