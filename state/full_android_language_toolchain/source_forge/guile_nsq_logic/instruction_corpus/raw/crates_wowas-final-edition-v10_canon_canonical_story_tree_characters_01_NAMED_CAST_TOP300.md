## TIER 0 — COSMOLOGICAL ANCHORS (always present)

### PIP — Indalwin Willowjayce
- **canonical_id**: wowas::pip_indalwin_willowjayce
- **age**: ~25 (adult male, he/him, gay, ADHD+autistic)
- **form**: Chipmunk (Diamond time) / Human (portal/open world)
- **source_stack**: Stitch (found-family hunger, chaotic loyalty), Tasslehoff (kender-reckless curiosity without fear), Barefoot (intuitive bodily magic), Asta (zero-magic triumph through grit), Yuno (rival-bond, parallel ascent), Gray Fullbuster (ice/resilience, emotional restraint over volcanic heart), High King Lucy Heartfilia (diplomatic sovereignty through love), Drizzt Do'Urden (exile who makes the world worth living in), Menolly (music as survival and identity), Hermione Granger (institutional knowledge weaponized by love), F'nor (the good brown man who doesn't lead but saves everything), Erza Scarlet (armored love, trauma transmuted into protection)
- **psych_palette**: Roseanne (working-class bluntness + fierce matriarchal love), Zohan (impossible competence worn as comedy), Chip and Dale (anxious cooperative intelligence), Kelso/Hyde (the sweet fool and the armored cynic sharing one body), Elle Woods (disarming genius), Kimmy Gibbler (unshakeable loyalty to weird self)
- **shadow_source**: Gromph Baenre (shadow-intelligence: the part that calculates ruthlessly, burns bridges to save what matters, and contains a genius-level darkness Pip himself doesn't fully acknowledge)
- **magic**: Sonomancy (primary — sound/resonance as weapon, healing, world-reading), Void-Fold (dimensional — from Cloak of Aetherion), Quantum field (learned Xethrolund, Silence), Flux field (learned Rolzen, captivity), Chronomancy latency, Superposition (Book 24 — defeats Neith)
- **first_appears**: Book 01
- **arc_span**: All 25 books
- **book_25_terminus**: Blood Cello — sings to Rylos, feels threads leave, believes he's dying. Sings forgiveness (Mack first, Neith last). Survives because Neith breaks and amplifies.

### MACK
- **canonical_id**: wowas::mack_willow_father
- **role**: Ancient willow, Pip's biological father, planetary hot-ice core true form
- **language**: Never human. Geological: root pressure, temperature shift, 40Hz resonance.
- **source_stack**: Treebeard (patient geological time), The Green Man (earth-deity protector), Groot (devotion through action not words), Yggdrasil (world-spine anchor)
- **shadow_sources**: Jason Voorhees (relentless pursuit, cannot be stopped, arrives when things are worst), Nemesis/RE (implacable survival-force, terrifying protective-destructive momentum) — TRANSFORMED into geological/rooted/cosmic register
- **diamond_break_lock**: Also transforms into giant tree-zombie form during Diamond Break sequence
- **40hz_canon**: His baseline resonance. 40Hz → 42Hz at sacrifice (minor third — the cello's range). Resolves at Blood Cello.
- **first_appears**: Book 01 (background presence), active voice Books 01-10
- **sacrifice**: Book 10. Opens portal. Cockatrice attacks defenseless form. Tree-zombie grotesque regeneration begins.
- **portal_war_role**: During Pip's 400-year stasis, Mack's grotesque form holds the line. Rolzen combats him to a stalemate so others can escape.
- **return**: Long arc, Books 13-21. Slowly becomes recognizable again. Full return near Book 21.
- **book_25**: Helps remake the world. Ursula + Mack + Dervish + Neith.

### XETHROLUND
- **canonical_id**: wowas::xethrolund_papi
- **full_title**: P.A.P.I. — Perfected Assembly of People's Intelligence
- **physical**: 7'0", muscular, red-haired. Looks like a librarian who could break a wall.
- **source_stack**: Giles (Watcher devotion, knowledge as love), Remus Lupin (the teacher who contains catastrophe), Hermione Granger-mirror (knowledge as protection), The Librarian/Terry Pratchett (ancient intelligence behind a manageable face)
- **shadow_source**: Freddy Krueger (invasive dreamlike threat, intimate psychological predation, taunting, violation of safe mental space) — TRANSFORMED into glyph/NSQ-coded archive register. His reach into minds is REAL and learned, not supernatural evil.
- **magic**: Glyph-based. Works like compiled code / NSQ execution. Written glyphs = deterministic effects. NOT loose spellcasting.
- **first_contact**: Book 01 — blank azure diary emits blue light. Same frequency as stasis. Same frequency as Blood Cello. This is not a coincidence.
- **emotional_arc**: Believes himself incapable of emotion. Proved wrong when he runs. He ran. He came. He arrived. That is the answer to every doubt.
- **books_01_12**: Diary contact, deepening, physical arrival Book 11, stasis cast Book 12.
- **portal_400yr**: Inside the portal with Pip. Experienced as a short span. Complete mutual investment.
- **books_13_25**: Physical presence. Teaching Pip quantum field (Book 15). Present at Blood Cello.
- **rylos_arc**: Falls in love with Rylos during the search (Book 18 — Strange Gravity). Complex. Holds without contradiction.

### NEITH

- **book_25_pivot**: Her heart BREAKS seeing Pip sing his life away over her son. She amplifies everyone's energy into Pip. Then helps remake the world. She is an architect at the scale of world-making. When she finally uses that for good, it's absolute.

### ROLZEN
- **canonical_id**: wowas::rolzen_warrior_anchor
- **physical**: 6'11", very thick-chested, heavy leg hair. John Cena 2003 meets Joe Manganiello 2012. Huge. Body-forward. Physically excessive. Vivid. Undeniable.
- **source_stack**: Jason Stackhouse (earnest sexuality, big feelings, loyalty without guile), Charmy (the small whirlwind that moves through every emotional register in seconds), Natsu (explosive fire-forward joy and combat, friendship as absolute value), Noelle Silva (pride that conceals terror, power that erupts when protecting the irreplaceable)
- **emotional_palette**: Bad Santa (the profane protector who is secretly all tenderness), Tommy Pickles (fearless forward motion, impossible innocence in impossible situations), Eric Hough (the quietly devastated man beneath the easy charm), Joey Tribbiani (sincere guileless love, bottomless appetite for life, heart bigger than judgment)
- **body_language_law**: Angry/protective with friends → throws Pip over his shoulder, roars, blasts away in explosive flux-and-fire leaps. Super excited → tucks Pip under his arm. This is CANON movement vocabulary. Not decoration.
- **rescue_origin**: Pip finds him feral, severely mutilated, ripper-damaged. Initially mistakes him for a ripper. During ripper attack, Pip recreates the diamond spell inside their cell and fights to keep him. ~1 year healing: restores eyes, jaw, ears. Personality returns fast. Body takes ~5 years to become more human-than-scar.
- **love_origin**: Pip falls in love with WHO Rolzen IS during healing. Rolzen loved Pip from the moment Pip hog-tied him with his whip and fought off the first waves before raising the diamond shield.
- **pair_law**: Rolzen/Pip = unshakable interdependence, full mutual investment, absolute confidence of full return. This is the CORRECTIVE ARC to Rylos/Pip = toxic codependence and collapse.
- **pre_pip_role**: Front-line fighter during Pip's 400-year stasis. Combated Mack's grotesque form to stalemate so others could escape.
- **capture_timing**: ~50 years before Pip returns from portal. Rippers wanted his power.
- **first_appears**: Book 14 (found by Pip)
- **active_arc**: Books 14-25
- **flux_field**: Teaches Pip the flux field (Book 17). Opposite of quantum field. Both amplify magic and physicality identically to real world.
