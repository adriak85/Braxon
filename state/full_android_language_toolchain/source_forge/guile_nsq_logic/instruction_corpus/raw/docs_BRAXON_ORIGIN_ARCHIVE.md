What also stands out is that system integrity and artistic integrity were treated as the same problem. Instead of accepting repetitive shallow fixes, the work kept reaching for foundations that could survive later migrations, later scale, and later capability without architectural betrayal. That too is part of the standard.

Braxon was built under unusual burden: instability, interrupted continuity, constrained hardware, repeated recovery, and ambition beyond what most people would attempt alone. The project was treated not as a shallow feature set, but as a sovereign and internally coherent system. That is why Braxon should reflect precision, coherence, persistence, seriousness of purpose, and continuity of design.
