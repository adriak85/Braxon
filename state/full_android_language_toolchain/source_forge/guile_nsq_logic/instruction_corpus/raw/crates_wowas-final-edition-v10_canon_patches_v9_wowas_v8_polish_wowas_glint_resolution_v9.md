## Resolution of previously unresolved identity

Glint is a Veil-Walker trainee who was present at the Glass Palace for non-party reasons: he was mapping dimensional plane access points in the area, which the Veil-Walkers do routinely in zones with heavy sonomantic activity. The Glass Palace had been generating unusual dimensional echoes for months, which is what brought him there independently.

At the B09 choice trap sequence, he witnesses:
- Pip's forced soul restoration (the choice itself)
- Boojay's siphon mechanism exposed
- The Diamond's nature as battery confirmed in real time

**What he does with what he saw**: He returns to the Veil-Walkers with a full dimensional report. The report is accurate and damning and the Veil-Walkers do not know what to do with it because it contradicts their existing models of the Diamond's function. The report sits in their archive for eleven years before anyone retrieves it.

**His arc**: He appears once in Book 09, once in Book 14 (when Pip emerges and Glint's report finally gets retrieved), and once in Book 17 (during the Silence corridor — he is the Veil-Walker trainee who has, in the intervening years, become the person who finds dimensional access points that shouldn't exist). He is not a major character. He is the person whose accurate report was ignored until it was too late to ignore.

**Source stack**: Neville Longbottom (the quiet one who was there for all of it); Sam Gamgee (the witness who carries the story forward)  
**Moral texture**: He did the right thing perfectly and it made no immediate difference. He has made peace with this. He finds the peace unsatisfying and correct.

**Physical**: Average height, mid-range Veil-Walker build, carries a dimensional-sense instrument that looks like a tuning fork with too many tines. The instrument has not been wrong once in eleven years.
