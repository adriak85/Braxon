# WoWaS Books 24–25 Priority Override Patch v10

## Purpose
This patch forces `Born To Die` and `Death Is Rebirth` to resolve manuscript debt through their highest-value lived scene classes first.

It exists because the final books currently show repeated scaffold pressure through:
- death law / rebirth law placeholders
- tone and structure lock placeholders
- repeated reconstruction filler
- abstract equation-style scene titles

These books must not be expanded by generic connective summary.
They must cash out through accountability, wound, waking, changed-return, fragile joy, and lived rebirth.

Use with:
- `patches/PROSE_AND_TONE_GUIDE.json`
- `patches/v10/wowas_dialogue_and_play_insertion_patch_v10.md`
- `patches/v10/wowas_book_dialogue_seed_registry_v10.md`
- `patches/v10/wowas_anti_placeholder_expansion_patch_v10.md`

## Scope
This override applies first and most strongly to:
- Book 24 — `Born To Die`
- Book 25 — `Death Is Rebirth`

When any scene in these books is still scaffold-led, this override takes precedence over ordinary connective expansion.

## Core law
The final books must spend manuscript energy on:
- emotional truth
- consequence
- embodiment
- relational change
- proof that return is real
- proof that joy survives catastrophe

They must not spend their best pages on abstract summary.

## Book 24 — Born To Die
The dominant manuscript burden of Book 24 is not spectacle.
It is:
- accountability
- mortal injury
- grief
- impossible choice
- emotional legibility under catastrophic scale

### Book 24 priority order
When expanding Book 24, prefer in this order:

1. Accountability scenes  
   - Rylos / Pip truth named clearly
   - Neith's role named clearly
   - no cheap absolution
   - no flattening into vague tragedy

2. Mortal-wound scenes  
   - the wound must be carried through bodies, speech, reaction, and relational fracture
   - injury is not only event; it is lived consequence

3. Superposition / impossible-act scenes  
   - Pip's action must be emotionally and morally legible
   - readers must understand not only what he did, but why he could do it

4. Grief and aftermath speech  
   - at least some of the devastation must be borne in dialogue
   - the survivors must sound like people, not just witnesses to myth

5. Quiet ruin scenes  
   - after catastrophic scale, give specific speech, touch, silence, or recognition
   - these scenes prove the loss landed

### Book 24 placeholder mappings
Use these conversions:

- `The Book Finds Its Death Law`
  ->
  accountability / mortal-wound / grief / impossible-cost scene

- `Ghost = Obligation`
  ->
  memory now demanding action or witness

- `Presence ≠ Solved`
  ->
  survivors still present to one another but not emotionally repaired

- `Nearness = Terminus`
  ->
  closeness becoming the edge where loss, wound, or truth must finally be named

- `The Cello Was Built From Consequence`
  ->
  a scene that makes accumulated cost audible, personal, and lived

- `Every Ghost Was Present For The Playing`
  ->
  speech, perception, or felt-company scene where the dead and nearly-dead remain part of the emotional field

- `Pip Knew What Had To Be Given`
  ->
  choice scene with moral clarity and bodily cost

- `The Last Approach Was Agreement`
  ->
  final acceptance or alignment scene, not abstract thematic summary

## Book 25 — Death Is Rebirth
The dominant manuscript burden of Book 25 is not merely restoration.
It is:
- waking
- changed embodiment
- proof of survival
- return with consequence
- fragile delight
- obligation to live again

### Book 25 priority order
When expanding Book 25, prefer in this order:

1. Waking / proof-of-life scenes  
   - bodily proof first: breath, ground, pain, touch, confusion, hunger, trembling, sound
   - survival must be felt before it is explained

2. Changed-return scenes  
   - what returned did not return unchanged
   - the cast must say so or show so in concrete terms

3. Rebirth-as-obligation scenes  
   - survival creates duty, not just relief
   - the living owe something to the world and to one another

4. Fragile joy scenes  
   - food, laughter, relief, touch, dogs, beauty, awkwardness, tears, astonishment
   - these are structural proof of successful rebirth

5. Lived-world rebuilding scenes  
   - the reborn world must feel habitable
   - not merely cosmically repaired, but livable

### Book 25 placeholder mappings
Use these conversions:

- `The Book Finds Its Rebirth Law`
  ->
  waking / changed-return / obligation / fragile joy scene

- `Farewell = Instrument`
  ->
  farewell carried through music and relational loss

- `Waking = Proof`
  ->
  bodily waking scene proving survival is real

- `Rebirth = Obligation`
  ->
  dialogue scene where return creates responsibility

- `The Song That Cut The World Open`
  ->
  aftermath scene where characters live inside the consequence of that act

- `What Returned Could Not Return Unchanged`
  ->
  explicit changedness scene in body, mind, bond, or world

## Mandatory embodiment rule
For both Books 24 and 25, expansion must prefer bodily reality over abstract theme.

At least one of these should appear in every major realization scene:
- breath
- trembling
- tears
- blood
- collapse
- nausea
- touch
- heartbeat
- silence that means something
- the sound of another person surviving nearby
- hunger, thirst, exhaustion, or relief
- altered voice or changed movement

## Mandatory dialogue rule
For both Books 24 and 25, at least one major expansion scene per placeholder cluster must include natural spoken dialogue.

Not summary of what was said.
Actual exchange.

## Anti-spectacle rule
Do not let scale replace humanity.

Bad expansion:
- cosmic event summary with emotional labels

Better:
- a person trying to speak after waking
- someone laughing once and then crying because the laugh exists
- someone naming what changed in another person
- someone touching ground, food, fur, hand, or instrument as proof they are still here

## Cluster resolution rule
If multiple adjacent scenes in Book 24 or 25 carry the same placeholder burden, do not resolve them all the same way.

Instead distribute them across:
- reckoning
- wound
- grief
- waking
- changedness
- obligation
- fragile joy
- lived rebuilding

## Emergency override
If the generator faces a choice between:
- another connective summary scene
or
- a lived accountability / wound / waking / joy scene

it must choose the lived scene.

## Outcome rule
After applying this patch:

Book 24 should read like catastrophe became personal truth.  
Book 25 should read like survival became lived obligation and fragile joy.

The reader should finish the pair feeling:
- the cost was real
- the return was real
- the people were still people
- joy survived honestly
