- Quantum is Xethrolund's line.

- Blood Cello blackout is the midpoint of Book 25, not the ending.
- During Blood Cello Pip is narrowed into playing, not explaining.
- Rylos follows accountability, not cheap redemption.

## Character-specific corrective law

### Pip
- Tight third-person limited remains Pip-only.
- The reader should know only what Pip knows, misread what Pip misreads, and feel the world through his embodied resonant perception.
- Pip is not slogan-like by default.
- Under pressure he solves constantly.
- In calm gaps he goes blank/receptive and reads resonance, pressure, pattern, and vibration before words.
- His dialogue varies by state:
  - danger: short and practical
  - hurt/overwhelmed: clipped, strange, or quiet
  - wonder/breakthrough: curious, specific, more talkative
- Pip expresses judgment without condemnation.
- Pip does not morally dither once the line is clear.
- Pip's failures come from mercy, overextension, hope, and overvaluing others, not stupidity.

### Rolzen
- Rolzen's magic is force / boom, not fire.
- Rolzen uses crystal balls, not sais.
- He builds spells through gravity juggling, not levitation.
- Default loadout is six 4-inch crystal spheres plus a larger anchor sphere.
- He can line them up kinetically, boom them, rebound them, redirect them through flips and kicks, and catch them back into pattern.
- Detonation costs the most energy.
- After detonation a sphere collapses to a bead that he catches and re-expands.
- He swallows the beads at the end of combat to store/recover them.
- He is warm, devoted, fascinated by Pip's tiny specific personhood, protective, and physically exuberant.
- His care often shows before his flirtation.

### Rylos
- Rylos must still carry charm, teaching warmth, and visible emotional intelligence.
- Manipulation arises through concealment and steering, not cartoon villain speech.
- He should read as apex Aries-coded fire.
- He must not be flattened into generic anger.

### Xethrolund
- Xethrolund's line must preserve cosmic recognition, precision, intimate impossible knowing, and the fear of unhealthy overfusion.
- His love of Pip's mind, clarity, and perception must remain visible.

### Kyreal
- Kyreal is a wolf, one of Pip's best friends, bipedal, sword-bearing, and stone-aligned.
- He must make school-life and class life feel inhabited.
- His scenes should carry best-friend energy, relief banter, and world-enjoyment.
