# WoWaS Canon v1

Status: single active canon-control surface.

This file is the active cohesive canon set.
The earlier control bundles and control-patch documents have been applied into this v1 master and are no longer meant to remain as separate active canon-control files.

---

## 1. Operating law
- This file is the one active canon-control surface for WoWaS.
- Canon truth should not be split across multiple control bundles serving the same purpose.
- Patch-law from prior control documents is considered applied here.
- Scene-tree rewrite and derived-index regeneration should proceed from this v1 canon.
- Tight third-person remains Pip-only.
- The reader should know what Pip knows, misread what Pip misreads, and feel the world through his embodied resonant perception.

---

## 2. Manuscript law
- There is no prewritten manuscript route; canon produces manuscript.
- Each beat should normally become its own chapter.
- Expand through concrete sensation, dialogue, play, consequence, world texture, and aftermath.
- Pip is joyful, artistic, curious, honest, disciplined, and emotionally raw, but not slogan-like.
- Pip remains recognizably human: tired, hungry, embarrassed, awkward, blunt, playful, grieving, confused, affectionate, or intensely clear as needed.
- Pip's inner dialogue exists, but felt resonance, pressure, pattern, and sensation usually arrive before words.
- Under pressure Pip solves constantly.
- In danger his dialogue is short and practical.
- In hurt or overwhelm his dialogue becomes clipped, strange, or quiet.
- In wonder or breakthrough he becomes unusually curious, specific, and talkative.
- Pip expresses judgment without condemnation.
- Pip does not morally dither once the line is clear.
- Pip's failures come from mercy, hope, overextension, and overvaluing others, not stupidity.

---

## 3. Scene authority law
Scene density is not completion.

Prefer, in order:
1. concrete placed scene files
2. direct source scene extracts with lived event wording
3. concrete actual-source scenes
4. concrete missing-scene anchors with unique relational or event value
5. interpreted abstract shorthand only after realization into lived scene content
6. reconstruction filler only as debt markers

The following do not count as completed manuscript scenes by themselves:
- source-derived reconstruction
- repeated reconstructed continuity scene rows
- rewritten beat end
- rewritten from detail
- rewritten book open
- editorial status language
- equation-style placeholders
- count-padding continuity rows

A shorthand or scaffold row becomes real scene authority only when realized into lived scene content with at least three of:
- bodily sensation
- spoken dialogue or meaningful silence
- visible action or choice
- relational movement
- setting texture
- emotional consequence
- aftermath carryover

If multiple rows are the same scene-family with title drift or bookkeeping variation, compress them into one family and build from the strongest anchor.

Book build order must be driven by scene quality and distinct event value, not row count.
A dense scaffold family may not dominate selection merely because it is numerous.

Strong-anchor quota for manuscript-facing selection:
- at least 60% of selected rows should be strong concrete anchors
- no more than 25% may be editorial shorthand shaping rows
- no more than 10% may be reconstruction debt markers
- debt markers never count as completed prose coverage

## 4. Anti-placeholder law
Placeholder phrasing must not survive into reader-facing prose.
These are generator debt, not final scene text:
- tone and structure lock
- the book finds its law / death law / rebirth law / resonance law
- reconstructed continuity scene ####
- filled to target scene count using uploaded source anchors
- equation-style placeholders left uninterpreted

The generator must convert placeholder burden into lived scenes, not paraphrase the placeholder.

## 5. Core canon locks
- Only Pip is captured first.
- Pip is 25.
- Portal / stasis duration is 400 years under Xethrolund.
- Rolzen, not Rosen.
- Daisy May and Majiskii are separate Great Danes.
- Rylos is the operative primary name; Boojay remains only legacy alias/source trace.
- Xethrolund's magic is glyph-compiled deterministic NSQ execution.
- Rylos follows accountability, not redemption.
- Blood Cello blackout is the midpoint of Book 25, not the ending.
- During Blood Cello Pip is narrowed into playing, not explaining.
- Rolzen and Rylos are distinct and must never be conflated.
- Main cast is adult-coded.
- Rylos darkness must not telegraph early; betrayal lands near the Diamond Break.

## 6. Pip / Rolzen captivity, recovery, and bond law
- Rolzen is discovered inside captivity, not co-captured from outside.
- Pip discovers Rolzen feral, mutilated, and nearly ripper-like.
- Pip survives by deflecting rather than striking and begins flooding Rolzen with healing through those deflections.
- Pip recreates the diamond inside the cell and spends about a year healing Rolzen enough for escape.
- Rolzen's personality returns faster than his body.
- Their bond forms under captivity, healing, defense, escape, and recovery pressure.
- Pip loved Rolzen even while Rolzen still looked grotesque and monstrous.
- Rolzen never truly broke; captors only fully turned people after breaking them first.
- Once Pip gets Rolzen back to himself, Rolzen becomes recognizably human in demeanor quickly.
- Rolzen flirts from early recovery onward, expecting refusal.
- Nearly five years later, after difficult escape, Pip finally answers: "if you want to."
- When Rolzen breaks the impossible to protect them, Pip's only thought is: "that's what it looks like," and he does not understand why he is crying again as he falls helplessly in love.
- After that breakthrough, the first thing Pip does is hug and love on Daisy May and Majiskii, smile big, and become unusually talkative.
- Rolzen's explanation of Flux: he saw what he would have to do, focused so hard on that outcome that the world seemed to yield, almost freeze, and leave him as the only thing still moving.

## 7. Pip sonomancy law
- Pip practices sonomancy constantly in secret.
- He hides the scale of it from the world.
- Rolzen knows Pip's mastery long before the world does because he has directly witnessed the private truth of Pip's work.
- Before Blood Cello scale events, sonomancy is expressed only rarely.
- During Blood Cello, Pip is not explaining or strategizing as his primary mode; he is narrowed into playing.

## 8. Xethrolund / Rylos search lane
- While Pip is gone, Xethrolund and Rylos search for him separately and then together.
- Their shared search becomes its own arc.
- They help one pristine, wealthy, beautiful civilization that collapses in their hands.
- They help one poorer, defensive, loving, mutually protective civilization that becomes stronger.
- A Saudi Arabian / American-born gravity-time healer mage intermittently joins them and helps heal old wounds in conversation.

## 9. Oregon -> Idaho -> Onondaga -> Mississippi corridor law
1. The party reaches the Oregon coast by Pip's oceanic dream.
2. The sea takes only Pip.
3. Pip returns with two embedded stones no one can remove.
4. The dream sharpens into stone lily pads / cave-water / giant cavern imagery.
5. Idaho provides brochure proof and forces a Missouri route.
6. The stones protrude into geodes on the way to Onondaga.
7. The geodes become a neon-pink dragon-shell around Pip.
8. Three-day shell siege under ripper pressure.
9. Pip emerges in a gemstone storm with shell-born geometric wings.
10. Zombie Mack attacks immediately.
11. The party loses Mack down to Mississippi.
12. Rylos is pulled into his own shadow at the crater site.
13. Pip's roar reaches him as love-confirmation.
14. Rylos answers with the lemon-sun awakening and plasma bow-shot into his own shadow.

## 10. Demon / angel war lane
- After Pip and Rolzen escape they encounter demons and angels at war.
- They observe before taking a side.
- The angels are attempting extermination because the others are demons, not because the demons are the true aggressor.
- Pip and Rolzen side with the demons against extermination.
- After victory, the purple demon telecaster joins the group.
- An angelic Cantonese woman with a fairy girlfriend on her shoulder eventually joins after being broken silent on the battlefield when her repression-breaking power collides with Pip's stronger emotional force.

## 11. Dragon Gates / Quantum / Flux law
- Dragon Gates are thresholds that both Pip and Rylos must open.
- Quantum is Xethrolund's line: threshold precision, state breach, impossible action under wrong conditions.
- Flux is Rolzen's line: adaptive force continuity, motion through change, living pressure transfer.
- Both Quantum and Flux are required for a survivable Neith victory.
- If either is missing, a battle-winning state may still collapse into total fatality.

## 12. School-era and Kyreal law
- Kyreal is a wolf, one of Pip's best friends, bipedal, sword-bearing, and stone-aligned.
- In the school-like survival-magic environment, Kyreal is the strongest in class in visible performance.
- Pip is already more powerful than Kyreal but does not understand that yet.
- Rylos and Pip both work harder than anyone because they are visibly weaker in that school-era hierarchy.
- Rylos compensates by learning to be quiet as a shadow.
- Pip compensates by obsessive practice.
- Kyreal later talks Pip into coming out with him to help him forget Rylos.
- Books 01–03 must preserve school life, friendship warmth, Kyreal benchmark pressure, diary/prophecy/Xeth contact, Pip curiosity, and Mack presence.

## 13. Diamond, Ursula, Vaedric, and shell law
- The Diamond was conjured to steal Pip and Rylos's power.
- Mack intervened by holding the Diamond back.
- The combined energy of Mack + Pip + Rylos filling the Diamond is what gave the other trapped people power.
- The same Diamond state turned the trapped people into animals while inside it.
- In portal state Pip and Rylos become human because they are no longer under that same Diamond expression law.
- The gemstone dragon-egg / shell event belongs only to Pip.
- The shell must first read as fear, obstruction, and impossible transformation.
- Ursula is not physically seen until the party reaches the core of her realm.
- Ursula reveals Pip's parentage as Ursula + Mack.
- Ursula persists in geode-form; Mack is the hot ice core.
- Ursula gives Pip an amethyst crystal that can end Mack's failing current life so he can return to core-state.
- Vaedric Maldor Obstineth is the Cockatrice.
- He establishes a coercive trauma bond with Rylos, keeps him hidden from Mack, opens the void with the red obsidian blade, and later returns once as a ripper who deals Rylos the mortal blow.

## 14. Silence / Lucent / Ursula / traversal law
- In the Silence, the air is wrong and wings do not generate lift.
- Glide fails there.
- Glide returns in Lucent / Ursula realm after the stone break and rewind.
- Breaking Ursula's stone returns the party to the surface in a near-rewind to just before entry.
- Items acquired inside disappear.
- Scars gained inside are reversed.
- Kyreal's death remains real.
- In the hardest traversal sections, Pip must first reach stable footing before he can lay a path back for the others.
- Whip traversal pre-exists the update-10 transfiguration lane.
- Glide belongs to the transfigured post-angel weapon state.

## 15. Rolzen combat law
- Rolzen is force / boom, not fire.
- He uses crystal balls, not sais.
- His combat system is a spell-through-motion lattice built from gravity juggling crystal spheres.
- Default pattern: six 4-inch spheres plus one larger top sphere.
- He can line them up kinetically, strike the line, launch one, boom on impact, rebound, recatch, and re-enter the juggle.
- He can redirect multiple spheres through flips, kicks, tumbles, and changing vectors.
- He can time ground booms to suspend himself over opponents briefly.
- Detonation is highest-cost release.
- A detonated sphere collapses to a bead, which he catches and re-expands later.
- At the end of combat he swallows the beads.

## 16. Weapon and transfiguration law
- After the attack on the angels, Pip converts the dead into diamonds.
- The amethyst rises, summons the diamonds, drains the charge out of Pip's staff until it falls back into an inert twig, and returns the power in form.
- Good alignment path: brilliant clear staff with violet amethyst at both ends.
- Evil alignment path: crimson blood-gem greatsword with weight and damage-over-time signature.
- Over-neutrality path: no transformation.
- The awakened weapon takes on a life of its own.
- Staff path retains split function into tomahawks or whips.
- Sword path can break at the handle to preserve partial whip functionality.

## 17. Travel, monster, and threat law
- Travel may not relax into connective tissue.
- Every corridor must feel inhabited, dangerous, consequential, and worth surviving.
- Encounters should combine route pressure, terrain pressure, visibility pressure, and moral pressure.
- Every significant fight should feel edge-of-seat because the hostile field is doing more than one thing at once:
  - terrain sabotage
  - visibility distortion
  - route denial
  - mob coordination
  - attrition and exhaustion
  - rescue or moral pressure
  - species interaction shifts
- Monsters must not be flavor.
- They must be able to wound the party badly, split the party, endanger civilians, destroy shelter, force detours, convert wonder into danger, or make correct choices still cost something.
- Monster generation uses the same pressure-translation logic as character generation.
- Start from American flora, fauna, insects, arachnids, arthropods, microbes, fungi, fish, reptiles, amphibians, birds, mammals, and stones.
- Give each species 2–4 source-media pressures translated through WoWaS-native consequence.
- Keep American/common naming first, neo-scientific naming second.

## 18. Dialogue, play, and lived-world law
Dialogue and play are load-bearing.

When expanding canon into chapters:
1. preserve the beat
2. insert the strongest dialogue truth scene
3. insert the strongest lived-world / play / domestic scene
4. carry aftermath and emotional consequence
5. only then add connective narration

Mandatory scene classes include:
- relational truth scenes
- curiosity scenes
- domestic recovery scenes
- play scenes
- moral-pressure scenes
- sensory-first scenes

The cast must still:
- speak
- tease
- eat
- rest
- joke
- flirt
- wonder
- enjoy beauty
- recover together
- love the dogs
- keep joy alive under pressure

Rolzen's first meaningful act of magic in nearly a century is to feed Pip.
The first breakfast magic must feel like tenderness, strangeness, and a beginning.

## 19. Prison-break and Flux discovery law
- The prison-break is a true trap.
- If the player delays, explores, loots, or tries to save extra people here, someone dies.
- Once control returns, the door clicks and Pip must move immediately.
- Pip charges the door and blasts it the instant it opens enough.
- Daisy May and Majiskii surge forward without hesitation and choose the correct route.
- The only everyone-survives route requires immediate aggression, enough hand-to-hand skill, correct evade timing, correct crowd-control order, and correct response to Rolzen's timing cues.
- When Rolzen shouts "jump," obedience has only seconds of margin.
- Flux first reads as impossible fragmentation of Rolzen's position and then an explosion of crystal force.
- The deeper truth is that Rolzen did the impossible because Pip, the dogs, and the others were worth it to him.

## 21. Quality law
Canon accuracy alone is not enough.
Every meaningful beat, scene, character use, creature behavior, and action sequence should be checked for:
1. clarity
2. impact
3. memorability
4. lived feeling
5. public read
6. world-survival logic
7. emotional consequence
8. reread value

Heated romantic scenes must be checked for:
- chemistry
- emotional specificity
- distinct body language
- changed trust / confidence / fear / bond law
- whether the scene changes the relationship afterward
- adult, intentional, alive read

Wholesome scenes must be checked for:
- warmth
- earned relief
- memory value
- domestic specificity
- humor or softness with real character weight
- proof that the world is worth saving

Birthdays and holidays are structural opportunities for:
- relationship contrast
- cultural texture
- ritual law
- emotional memory
- grief by comparison to lost normalcy
- proof that people still celebrate in a wounded world
- fragile joy after devastation

Rylos should read as apex Aries-coded fire: forceful, proud, direct, reactive, charismatic, dangerous, funny, hot, maddening, and alive, not generic anger.
Rolzen and Xethrolund must read as strong but different resonance matches to Pip.
