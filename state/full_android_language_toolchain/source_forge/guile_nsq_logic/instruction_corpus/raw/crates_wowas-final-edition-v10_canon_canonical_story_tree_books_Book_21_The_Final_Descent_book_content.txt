# Book 21 The Final Descent — Scene Blocks

Status: provisional working draft
Authority: best-fit inferred scene implantation pass

## Purpose
Provide direct insertable scene blocks that satisfy the already locked escalation, wake, support-cast, villain-texture, and weak-area requirements.

## Formatting note
These are not final prose chapters. They are canon scene blocks designed to be inserted, expanded, or fused into beat files and chapter surfaces.

## Scene Block 1 — Thread Pressure in the Descent
Placement: early-middle descent

Summary:
The descent collapses partially, and Pip feels living and failing life-threads directly for the first time at unbearable scale. This is not yet Blood Cello, but it is its bodily precursor. He does not gain mastery from this. He gains pain, scale, and unwilling intimacy with the survival of others.

Purpose:
- seed Book 25 thread perception
- turn descent into wound-chamber, not just environment
- make Pip’s burden more exact

Required details:
- physical collapse and life-thread perception happen together
- Pip’s humility remains intact; he does not treat this like power acquisition
- someone has to pull him back into motion

## Scene Block 2 — Sorra’s Care-Hole
Placement: after collapse survival, if Sorra line is used

Summary:
Sorra chooses triage continuity over self-preservation. The important thing is not only that she dies or is lost; it is that the group immediately feels what kind of world disappears when a healer like her is gone. Her wake must be practical, moral, and intimate.

Purpose:
- create a care-hole
- satisfy death/rebirth framework with specific wake
- diversify the kinds of late-book cost

Required details:
- someone survives because Sorra stays
- someone else understands instantly what has been lost
- no melodrama inflation; let function make it hurt

## Scene Block 3 — Nieth-Held Passage
Placement: later descent transition

Summary:
A passage that should have collapsed long ago still holds because Nieth’s destroyed-but-lingering weave remains active. The scene must make that coherence feel damaged, wrong, and necessary.

Purpose:
- prevent Nieth from staying abstract
- justify how catastrophe remains navigable long enough for convergence
- deepen the sense that the world is being held together by wounded remnants
