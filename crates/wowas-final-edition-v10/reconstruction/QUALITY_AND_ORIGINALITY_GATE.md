# WOWAS Quality / Originality Gate

This gate is applied to generated characters and creatures before promotion into the reconstructed world.

## Reject / rehash

A generated object is not promoted when its identity depends on:

- a recognizable existing fictional character;
- a recognizable living/recorded performer, artist, public figure, or branded property used as a direct character template;
- a copied franchise creature, location, terminology, or distinctive character combination;
- a thin reskin whose defining identity is still the source property;
- exploitative shock concepts or names specifically called out by the source authority as weak/tasteless.

The source registry itself contains fields named `source_media_register_1` through `_4` and currently contains recognizable external character/property references. Those references are treated as **generation provenance/contamination signals**, not as canonical identity material.

## Promotion standard

A replacement must stand on its own through:

1. original name and identity;
2. original morphology;
3. original behavior/ecology;
4. original world relationship;
5. original encounter/quest function;
6. source-native geography or world-state relevance;
7. no dependence on the recognizable source property.

## Character review

The same gate applies to the 5,000-character expansion. Background characters can be simple, but they must still be independently identifiable and useful to their atlas/orbit/world role rather than being disguised copies of recognizable characters.

## Quality selection

Prefer concrete, distinctive, internally coherent designs over quantity. Rehash weak candidates rather than padding counts. Do not promote a candidate merely because it fills a target slot.

## Provenance

Original source records are never silently erased. A rejected candidate remains auditable in the reconstruction ledger with its source path and reason for rejection; only its **active-world promotion** is removed.
