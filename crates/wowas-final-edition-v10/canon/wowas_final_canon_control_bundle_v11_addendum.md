# WoWaS Final Canon Control Bundle v11 Addendum

Status: authoritative addendum layered after v10.

## 1) Pip cognition / dialogue law
Pip's mind is never restful. Under pressure he is constantly solving to keep himself and those he loves in the best condition he can. In rare calm gaps, he goes blank/receptive and reads energies, presence, and natural vibration as more revealing than speech. This tendency is deepened by Daisy May and Majiskii. His dialogue should follow pressure-state:
- active danger: short, practical, solution-driven
- hurt / overwhelmed: clipped, strange, or quiet
- breakthrough / wonder: suddenly specific, curious, and unusually talkative

## 2) Prison-break / Flux discovery emotional law
When Rolzen breaks the impossible to protect them, Pip's only thought is:
"that's what it looks like"
He does not understand why he is crying again as he falls helplessly in love.

## 3) Immediately after Flux discovery
Once clear, the first thing Pip does is hug and love on Daisy May and Majiskii and smile big. This is the first time he becomes particularly talkative. He starts asking Rolzen everything about what he did and how he did it.

Rolzen's explanation:
He saw what he would have to do, focused so hard on that outcome that the world seemed to yield, almost freeze, and leave him as the only thing still moving.

## 4) Eggs / Majiskii / Daisy May room law
When Pip takes his first bite of eggs, Majiskii and Daisy May abruptly appear in the room as if signaled by the eggs in his mind.
The first spoken line in the room is Rolzen saying:
"So you must be Majiskii!"
Pip had mentioned her name so quickly earlier that it barely registered before everything moved on, so Rolzen is simply shocked and blushes.

Important correction:
Pip, not Rolzen, is the one who briefly forgets where he is. The instant he sees Daisy May and Majiskii, the full peril returns all at once. Nothing in the world matters more to him than those two, and he knows he cannot make them go back.

Majiskii remains calm, sweet, lazy, and unexpectedly powerful/brave.
Daisy May stretches huge and both settle by Pip.

## 5) Prison-break route law
The prison-break is a true trap:
- if the player tries to save extra people here, someone dies
- if the player explores, someone dies
- if the player stops to loot, someone dies

Once control returns after the cutscene, the player hears the door click.
If the player does not immediately move, they are already late.

Pip hears the click and charges the door.
The instant the hinge opens enough, he blasts it with his hardest spell.
Six rippers in the way die instantly.
There are still roughly ten more in the hall.
Pip clears the left side first; by the time he turns, the others are already gone.

Daisy May and Majiskii surge forward without hesitation and choose the correct route.
The route is correct because the dogs chose it. Any other direction is wrong because it is not where they took the party.

## 6) Escape gauntlet gameplay law
The only route where everyone survives requires:
- immediate aggression
- enough hand-to-hand skill
- correct evade timing
- correct crowd-clearing order
- correct response to Rolzen's timing cues

Failure bands:
- if the first major group is not fully cleared fast enough, Rolzen does not follow and the player is overwhelmed
- if the second group is not controlled correctly, enemies peel onto the dogs or back onto Rolzen
- if the player is spit-stunned once, they are overwhelmed
- when Rolzen shouts "jump," the player has about three seconds to obey

Then:
- a low blast takes the legs out from under the enemy wave
- the player enters a high-speed obstacle race
- wall-climb
- gust ride
- near-fall
- then a crystal ball appears under Pip's feet for one last jump
- after landing, Pip summons the path for the others

As Pip climbs the wall, rippers are dropping onto him trying to tear his head off. Dodging left/right is required with almost no margin. A half-second mistake is failure.

## 7) Flux visual law
When Flux is first discovered, Pip's perception of Rolzen is fragmented:
Rolzen seems to go forward and backward at the same time, left and right at the same time, everywhere at once. Then everything becomes a giant explosion of crystal force.
The deeper truth of the scene is that Rolzen did the impossible because Pip, the dogs, and the others were worth it to him.

## 8) Rolzen pre-break history
Rolzen was a gymnast daredevil performer before the world broke. He had a partner who began aging supernaturally while Rolzen's own aging seemed frozen; that partner quickly became wraith after the break.

The truth that haunted Rolzen most:
the only thing you cannot protect someone from is themself.

After losing his partner, Rolzen spent himself protecting survivors in New York by repeatedly drawing zombie Mack away from the city before it could hit them. He would take Mack down the coast, lose it, then boat back up. One day he returned to devastation—people mauled, ripped apart, or dissolved—and when he turned to leave he was surrounded. He fought as hard as he could and never stopped fighting until he was finally taken.

## 9) Neith breakthrough / Rylos knowledge law
About a century after Vaedric first found the altar and started waking it, manifestations intensified:
- first only rippers
- then stronger and stronger expressions of Neith breaking through

Only Rylos knew why.
He does not tell Xethrolund until years into the search for Pip.

## 10) Dialogue writing law for Pip
Pip should not sound polished-by-default.
He sounds most true when:
- he is trying to pin reality down precisely
- he gets irritated by vague explanations
- he reveals feeling by accident while chasing mechanism
- he becomes especially talkative only when wonder or impossible tenderness breaks through pressure
