## Mid-span structural locks

### Pip / Rolzen lane
- Only Pip is captured first.
- He is gone for decades.
- Inside captivity Pip discovers Rolzen in a mutilated, feral, nearly-ripper condition.
- Pip recreates the diamond within the cell and spends about a year healing Rolzen enough for escape.
- Rolzen's personality returns faster than his body.
- Their bond is formed under captivity, healing, defense, and escape pressure.

### Xeth / Rylos search lane
- While Pip is gone, Xethrolund and Rylos are separately searching for him.
- They run into each other while trying to find Pip.
- Their shared search becomes its own arc, not merely downtime.
- They try to help two civilizations:
  - one **pristine, wealthy, beautiful, and not self-protective enough**, which collapses in their hands
  - one **poor in resources but defensive, loving, and mutually protective**, which becomes the stronger civilization with help from the trio
- During these arcs, a **Saudi Arabian / American-born gravity-time healer mage** intermittently joins them, heals old wounds in conversation, and helps incite the Xeth/Rylos romance without becoming a fixed core bond center.
- During this same span, Pip and Rolzen are escaping back toward the real world.

### Oregon -> Idaho -> Onondaga -> Mississippi corridor
1. The party reaches the Oregon coast by Pip's oceanic dream.
2. The sea takes **only Pip**.
3. Pip returns with two embedded stones that no one can remove.
4. The dream sharpens into stone lily pads / cave-water / giant cavern imagery.
5. Idaho provides brochure proof and forces a Missouri route.
6. The stones protrude into geodes on the way to Onondaga.
7. The geodes become a neon-pink dragon-shell around Pip.
8. Three-day shell siege under ripper pressure.
9. Pip emerges in a gemstone storm with shell-born geometric wings.
10. Zombie Mack attacks immediately; the cave event is stress-tested, not celebrated.
11. The party loses Mack down to Mississippi.
12. Rylos is pulled into his own shadow at the crater site.
13. Pip's roar reaches him as love-confirmation.
14. Rylos answers with the lemon-sun awakening and plasma bow-shot into his own shadow.

### Demon / angel war lane
- After Pip and Rolzen escape they encounter demons and angels at war.
- They observe first, not knowing who is right.
- They investigate both civilizations.
- The angels are attempting to destroy the demons because they are demons, not because the demons are the actual aggressor.
- Pip and Rolzen ultimately side with the demons to protect them from extermination.
- After victory, the **purple demon telecaster** joins the group.
- An angelic Cantonese woman with a fairy girlfriend on her shoulder also eventually joins after being broken silent on the battlefield when her repression-breaking power collides with Pip's superior emotional force.

### Dragon Gates / quantum-state lane
- **Dragon Gates** are thresholds that **both Pip and Rylos must open**.
- **Xethrolund teaches quantum state** in his own lane; this belongs under Xeth, not generic magic drift.
- A **non-core party member** dies later; the death slot is **not** the angel lesbian, **not** the fairy girlfriend, **not** the gravity-time healer, and **not** Kade Prismglaer.
