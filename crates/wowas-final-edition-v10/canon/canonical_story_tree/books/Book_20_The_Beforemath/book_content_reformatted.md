## Scene Block 1 — Future-Loss Wake Field
Placement: after the first major stabilization attempt fails

Summary:
The cast enters a zone where grief appears to have arrived before the deaths themselves. Not ghosts. Not prophecies in a clean sense. Wake-pressure. Familiar absences begin to take shape around still-living people as if the world has already started mourning what it knows is near. Nieth’s damaged lingering weave is the reason the field can hold this impossible emotional state without breaking into nonsense.

Purpose:
- establish wake law before final trigger deaths
- make Nieth’s continuity pressure bodily and visible
- put the cast inside future grief without paying it off yet

Required details:
- at least one place setting, object, threshold, or touch appears “already missing”
- Oswyn, if present, recognizes this as a grief-structure violation, not a haunting in the ordinary sense
- no character fully understands it
- Pip feels it too strongly but cannot yet name the life-thread logic

## Scene Block 2 — Memorial Truth Under Distortion
Placement: after the first wake-field encounter

Summary:
A gathering attempts to name the dead, the missing, and the almost-lost in a way that will hold the group together. The memorial process fails halfway because the wake field starts speaking through ordinary ritual. The point is not spectacle. The point is that mourning itself is now unstable, and the living have to choose whether to continue naming one another anyway.

Purpose:
- load communal grief
- prepare Oswyn’s memorial-truth role if used
- show the world still worth restoring

Required details:
- names matter here
- silence matters here
- someone refuses to let the ritual stop
- a villain-linked consequence or ideology is visibly implicated

## Scene Block 3 — Villain Reflected by Wake
Placement: after memorial sequence

Summary:
A villain or antagonist who normalized harm is forced to stand in an environment where the cost of their decisions is emotionally visible. They are not redeemed here. They are exposed. The wake field does not lecture them; it confronts them by making consequence legible.

Purpose:
- seed later villain return texture
- prepare Blood Cello as revelation field
- make moral consequence tangible
