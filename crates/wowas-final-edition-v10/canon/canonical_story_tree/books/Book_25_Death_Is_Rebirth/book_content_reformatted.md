# Book 25 — Blood Cello — Blood Cello Escalation

Deliver the earned trigger death, farewell-playing, resurrection field, destruction-tempo support, and reborn-world waking without making rebirth cheap.

## governing laws
- Pip believes he is saying goodbye, not wielding known resurrection.
- The field answers grief before it is understood as miracle.
- Waking must proceed through disbelief, sensory proof, recognition, and only then acceptance.
- Daisy May and Majiskii must not collapse into interchangeable comfort.

## corrected waking-use law
When Pip wakes in the reborn world:
- Daisy May should function as immediate living proof: bodily affection, sweetness, nearness, loving interruption of disbelief.
- Majiskii should function as calmer reality-proof: gentler grounding, quieter reassurance, steadier contact that helps the world remain touchable.
- If Majiskii's markings are foregrounded, do so only for continuity, identification, anchoring, or recognition.

## Blood Cello itself
Blood-made instrument, ether-thread field, destruction as tempo, return as audible world-law, and rubble becoming new creation.

Pip believes he is only saying goodbye. Everyone else hears farewell before miracle. Afterward Pip wakes thinking he may be dead or insane.

Rylos Vayne Johnson / Boojay is the trigger death that begins Blood Cello.

## Blood Cello setup consequence
1. Rylos dies saving Pip and Blood Cello begins immediately.
2. The instrument must feel born from grief, not summoned as a tactic.
3. Returned life must feel morally and emotionally earned.
4. Waking cannot skip into relief.
5. Daisy May should help make life feel present again before Pip fully trusts what he sees.
6. Majiskii should help make reality feel stable enough to remain in.
7. Boojay recognition must arrive gradually enough to hurt.
8. Communal confirmation must follow sensory proof, not replace it.
9. Pip passes out believing he died.
10. Pip wakes and questions sanity in the reborn world.

## downstream consequence into later books / Blood Cello
The waking sequence must carry enough bodily and emotional truth that the reborn world feels merciful, shocking, and costly at once.
