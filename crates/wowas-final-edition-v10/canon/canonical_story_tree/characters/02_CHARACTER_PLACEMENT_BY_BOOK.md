## Book 01 — Choices Make World
**Active cast**: Pip, Mack, Xethrolund (diary), Solvaenkyr (background), Dervish (2 appearances)
**Introduced this book**: Pip, Mack, Xethrolund (diary contact), Solvaenkyr, Dervish, Daisy May (background, unnoticed), the Cockatrice (threat), Rylos/Boojay (planted — name only)

## Book 25 — Death Is Rebirth (Blood Cello terminus chapter)
**Active cast**: All core cast. Every named character who survived.
**POV sequence in Blood Cello**:
1. Pip sings to Rylos. Feels threads disappear from ether. Believes he's dying.
2. Song changes to goodbye and forgiveness. Starts with Mack.
3. When Mack's thread disappears from ether — Pip believes he's lost Mack too.
4. Last one he sings to: Neith. Forgiveness. A wish for her better life.
5. Neith arrives. Everyone expects her to finish what she started.
6. Her heart breaks. She sees Pip singing his life away for her son.
7. She amplifies and focuses everyone's energy into Pip.
8. She helps Ursula, Mack, and Dervish remake the world she originally wove.
9. Had Pip been evil enough, or old and misusing magic, or without enough support: she would have destroyed the world or made something nasty.
10. But he is Pip.
**Last sentence of Blood Cello chapter**: NO ADJECTIVES. Subject. Verb. Object. Like a bow across a string. The note sustains.
