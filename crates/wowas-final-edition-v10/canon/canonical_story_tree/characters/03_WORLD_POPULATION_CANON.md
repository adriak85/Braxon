# WoWaS World Population Canon
# ~2 Million Characters — Generation Rules and Tier Structure
# Self-correcting, canonical, cohesive.

---

## POPULATION OVERVIEW

### Pre-Diamond-Break (Books 01-09)
- **Diamond Prison interior**: ~5,000 survivors total
- **Faction breakdown**:
  - Ash-Born: 1,400 (militant, organized)
  - Iron Accord: 900 (practical, medical)
  - Resonance: 800 (knowledge, sonomancy)
  - Severed: 600 (isolation-coded, self-sufficient)
  - Meridian: 400 (healing, ethics)
  - Unbound: 350 (free agents, traders, wanderers)
  - Veil-Walkers: 150 (dimensional, liminal)
  - Unaffiliated: 400 (no faction loyalty)
- **Outside the Diamond**: The wider world. Mostly unknown until Book 09. Neith's realm. Silence biomes. Other territories.

### Post-Diamond-Break (Books 09-25)
- **World opens**: The Diamond shatters. The ~5,000 survivors suddenly have access to everything.
- **The returned**: World-birth sequence (Books 13-20). People who died come back. Starting small, building.
- **Target world population**: ~2 million total for the full post-break world.
- **Structure**: Most are never named in the books. They have lives, places, routines, pressures, social ties. The world supports lived experience and believable memory.

## POPULATION GENERATION RULES

### Tier Structure
```
Tier 0: Cosmological anchors (7 characters — Pip, Mack, Xeth, Ursula, Neith, Rolzen, Dervish)
Tier 1: Named recurring cast (~50 characters — active across multiple books)
Tier 2: Named world characters (~250 characters — quest givers, traders, faction members, place anchors)
Tier 3: Documented background (~5,000 characters — named, have lives, may appear once)
Tier 4: World population (~2 million — rule-generated, consistent, believable)
```

### Naming Rules
**Structure**: [Given name root] + [Clan marker] + [Lineage marker]
- Clan markers: Vel'Thane, Ash'Koral, Marso'len, On'Rylder, Thorn'Vayne, Sol'Keth, Wren'Ash, Cinder'Mark, Stone'Hold, Iron'Song
- Lineage markers: Iron-Song, Stone-Hayne, Ash-Cinder, Dark-Current, Willow-Jayce, Still-Water, Thorn-Grace, Bone-Root, Fire-Weight, Salt-Edge, Glass-Spine, Null-Heart

**Syllable-to-fate encoding** (soft rule, not absolute):
- Opening consonants heavy (K, D, G, Th) → weight-bearers, guardians, survivors
- Opening vowels → knowledge-seekers, healers, connectors
- Compound lineage markers → cross-faction heritage
- Single lineage marker → faction-pure

**IP-clean rule**: ALL characters must be 0-variant (no direct franchise carryover — archetypal function preserved, not name/appearance).

### Generation Algorithm (for Tier 3 and 4)

**Step 1: Role assignment**
Assign from required-role list based on location and era:
- Quest givers (information holders, mission providers)
- Traders (resource movers, market operators)
- Faction members (soldiers, healers, administrators, artisans)
- Place anchors (permanent residents of a location)
- Travelers (cross-faction, cross-location)
- Returned (world-birth characters — arrived after Book 13)

**Step 2: Source stack (2-4 sources)**
Draw from approved source list. Apply harvest rules:
- 1/3 of strong positive sources → morally mixed or antagonist characters
- Evil characters always have real good side
- Source provides: emotional register, narrative function, power style, relationship mode
- What must NEVER be copied: names, specific visual design, IP-protected phrases

**Step 3: Faction and location assignment**
- Pre-break: one of 8 factions + unaffiliated
- Post-break: faction + location + era of arrival in that location

**Step 4: Routine and pressure**
Every character, even Tier 4, has:
- A daily routine (what they do when nothing is happening)
- A primary pressure (what keeps them up at night)
- A social tie (who they'd die for, who they're afraid of)
- A secret (something they haven't told their faction)

## WORLD-BIRTH CHARACTERS (returned — Books 13-25)

**The world-birth rule**: Anyone who died during the collapse or its aftermath may return in the world-birth sequence. Returns are not simultaneous — they happen in waves across Books 13-20. Some returns are welcomed. Some are not. Some returned people are not fully themselves.

**RETURN TYPE 1: FULL RETURN** — Complete memory, full personhood, intact relationship to the world. These are the easy ones. They are not actually easy.

**RETURN TYPE 2: PARTIAL RETURN** — Memory gaps. Something missing. The person is there but some piece of them is not. Reth Null-Heart has noticed this. He hasn't named it yet.

**RETURN TYPE 3: LATE RETURN** — Arrives after everyone has processed their grief. The world has moved on. The returned person has not. This is the most complicated.

**RETURN TYPE 4: UNEXPECTED RETURN** — Someone returns that everyone thought was fine. Wasn't. The collapse had been killing them slowly and they only fully died recently. Arrives young.

**THE CORVETH EXCEPTION**: Thorn Ash'Koral returns (Type 1, or possibly Type 2 — deliberately ambiguous). Corveth's reckoning is not about whether Thorn is fully Thorn. It's about whether Corveth can be in the same room as Thorn without the decision he made being the only thing in the room.

## WORLD POPULATION GENERATION — FULL SCALE (2 million)

### Geographic Distribution (post-break)

**Zone 1: Former Diamond Interior** (~250,000 people)
- The survivors and their descendants
- First two generations after the break
- Highly organized, faction-coded
- Dense documentation, strong social memory

**Zone 2: The Near-World** (~500,000 people)
- Within two days' travel of the former Diamond boundary
- Mixed: some fled the Diamond before its creation, some arrived after the break
- Less organized, more independent
- Quest density: HIGH (most quest givers are here)

**Zone 3: The Middle-World** (~800,000 people)
- Three to ten days' travel from the former Diamond
- Largely unaware of the Diamond's history until recently
- Own governance structures, own problems
- Quest density: MEDIUM

**Zone 4: The Far-World** (~450,000 people)
- Beyond ten days' travel
- Some knowledge of the Diamond as legend
- First encounters with opened-world travelers are happening now (Books 13-19)
- Quest density: LOW but individual quests very high stakes

### Population Generation Matrix

For any Zone and Faction/Affiliation combination:
1. Generate NAME using syllable/clan/lineage rules
2. Assign ROLE from the role-need list for that zone/era
3. Assign ROUTINE (what they do when nothing is happening)
4. Assign PRIMARY PRESSURE (what is actively in tension in their life)
5. Assign SOCIAL TIE (who they'd die for / who they fear)
6. Assign SECRET (something not yet spoken aloud)
7. Assign RETURN STATUS (if post-break): None / Returner / Waiting-for-returner / Griever-who-got-someone-back / Griever-who-didn't

### The 2-Million Consistency Rules

**Rule 1: The world has been going on without Pip.**
Every person's story started before Pip arrived and continues whether he's present or not.

**Rule 2: No person exists only to serve the plot.**
Everyone has a reason to be where they are that doesn't involve the protagonist.

**Rule 3: Grief is distributed.**
Every person lost someone in the collapse or the 400-year aftermath. The grief shapes behavior in specific ways.

**Rule 4: Factions bleed.**
Nobody is 100% their faction. Everyone has a connection across faction lines that complicates their simple identity.

**Rule 5: The world-birth changes everything locally.**
In any area where a return has happened, every person in that area is affected by it — whether they knew the returned person or not.

**Rule 6: Magic is not neutral.**
Sonomancy, flux fields, quantum fields, dimensional planes — everyone has a relationship to these whether they use them or not. Fear, awe, practical use, ideological opposition.

**Rule 7: The opened world is still being discovered.**
Every person who lives in Zone 3 or 4 is living through the largest expansion of known-world in anyone's memory. This is traumatic and wonderful simultaneously.
