# WoWaS Source Hero Engine
# The character creation ore. Not direct imports — transformation raw material.
# Every source reused at least 3 times, never the same way.
# ~1/3 of positive sources used for morally mixed or antagonist characters.

---

## ENGINE RULES (from canon update)
1. Source-hero list is a DIRECT character creator engine input
2. Parse story/world for required roles, functions, place-based needs
3. Each needed character generated from 2-4 source heroes
4. Harvest: emotional register, narrative function, power style, relationship mode
5. What must NEVER be copied: names, specific visual design, IP-protected phrases
6. Every source reused at least 3 times, NEVER the same way
7. ~1/3 of strong positive source material used in evil or morally mixed characters
8. Evil characters always have a real good side

---

## APPROVED SOURCE STACK — Full Harvest Catalog

### HARVEST ENTRY FORMAT:
```
SOURCE: [Name] | FRANCHISE: [Origin]
FUNCTION_HARVESTED: What narrative/emotional role can be extracted
LIGHT_SIDE: What's genuinely admirable/useful
SHADOW_SIDE: What's dangerous or can be inverted
RELATIONSHIP_STYLE: How they bond/break bonds
POWER_STYLE: How they apply capability
MUST_NOT_COPY: What's too specific to the IP
USED_IN: [Characters who already draw from this source]
```

### STITCH
- **FRANCHISE**: Lilo & Stitch
- **FUNCTION**: The engineered weapon who chose love and family
- **LIGHT_SIDE**: Fierce loyalty, adaptability, joy in chaos, found-family ferocity
- **SHADOW_SIDE**: Designed for destruction, still has the capacity, occasionally terrifying in what he can do when truly threatened
- **RELATIONSHIP_STYLE**: Chosen family as absolute law. Will destroy anything that threatens what he chose.
- **POWER_STYLE**: Unpredictable, physically excessive, emotionally precise
- **MUST_NOT_COPY**: Blue alien appearance, Hawaiian setting, specific catchphrases
- **USED_IN**: Pip (primary source — found-family hunger, chaotic loyalty)
- **AVAILABLE_FOR**: Morally-mixed warrior character who chose one person absolutely; an antagonist who bonded to the wrong thing with the same ferocity

### TASSLEHOFF BURRFOOT
- **FRANCHISE**: Dragonlance
- **FUNCTION**: The curious heart that has no fear and therefore no wisdom about which things should be feared
- **LIGHT_SIDE**: Pure curiosity, complete absence of malice, tends to accidentally do the thing everyone was afraid to do
- **SHADOW_SIDE**: The fearlessness is also thoughtlessness; can cause catastrophe with genuinely good intent
- **RELATIONSHIP_STYLE**: Attaches fully, moves fast, expects others to keep up
- **POWER_STYLE**: Disrupts systems by not recognizing they're systems
- **MUST_NOT_COPY**: Kender race elements, specific Dragonlance setting markers
- **USED_IN**: Pip (kender-reckless curiosity without fear)
- **AVAILABLE_FOR**: A young world-explorer who keeps finding things that should have stayed found; a villain whose curiosity crossed into violation

### JASON STACKHOUSE
- **FRANCHISE**: True Blood
- **FUNCTION**: The earnest, sexually confident, emotionally huge man who loves without guile
- **LIGHT_SIDE**: Genuine warmth, loyalty without strategy, capacity for real love, disarming sincerity
- **SHADOW_SIDE**: The same earnestness means he's been used by almost everyone who ever wanted something from him
- **RELATIONSHIP_STYLE**: Falls completely, doesn't know how to do partial
- **POWER_STYLE**: Physical forward, emotional honest, runs toward problems
- **MUST_NOT_COPY**: Vampire setting elements, specific True Blood mythology
- **USED_IN**: Rolzen (earnest sexuality, big feelings, loyalty without guile)
- **AVAILABLE_FOR**: A faction soldier who keeps falling for the wrong people; a reformed villain whose reformation looks exactly like his old corruption (genuinely loving something terrible)

### CHARMY

- **LIGHT_SIDE**: Complete emotional availability, loyalty without condition, the person who shows up
- **SHADOW_SIDE**: Can be deployed — the earnestness is real but it can be weaponized by smarter people nearby
- **RELATIONSHIP_STYLE**: Immediate attachment, defies social hierarchy to stay close to who she chose
- **POWER_STYLE**: Erupts, overwhelming, surprising in its actual scale
- **MUST_NOT_COPY**: Black Clover magic system specifics, specific design elements
- **USED_IN**: Rolzen (explosive fire-forward joy, emotional register speed)
- **AVAILABLE_FOR**: A spy whose cover is genuine warmth (because it IS genuine warmth); a mentor character who connects to the protagonist through food and presence

### NATSU DRAGNEEL

- **POWER_STYLE**: Explosive, escalating, fire-fueled, gets stronger when people he loves are threatened

- **USED_IN**: Rolzen (explosive joy and combat, friendship as absolute value)

### NOELLE SILVA
- **FRANCHISE**: Black Clover
- **FUNCTION**: Pride that conceals terror, power that erupts when protecting the irreplaceable
- **LIGHT_SIDE**: Genuine extraordinary capability, love that won't admit it's love until it can't not, protective ferocity
- **SHADOW_SIDE**: The pride keeps her from receiving help; she can be isolated because she won't ask
- **RELATIONSHIP_STYLE**: Denial → emergency → full commitment. Skips the middle.
- **POWER_STYLE**: Water/containment magic → can become absolute force when stakes are total
- **MUST_NOT_COPY**: Water magic specifics, specific nobility system
- **USED_IN**: Rolzen (pride that conceals terror, erupts when protecting Pip)
- **AVAILABLE_FOR**: A faction leader who keeps their power hidden and then cannot; a villain who protects something real with terrible methods

### DRIZZT DO'URDEN
- **FRANCHISE**: Forgotten Realms / R.A. Salvatore
- **FUNCTION**: The exile who makes the world worth living in by the quality of his choices
- **LIGHT_SIDE**: Moral clarity under pressure, chosen ethics over inherited evil, warrior as protector
- **SHADOW_SIDE**: The isolation of being better than the environment that shaped you; can become self-righteous; the exile identity can calcify
- **RELATIONSHIP_STYLE**: Slow to trust, absolute once committed, would die before betraying a companion
- **POWER_STYLE**: Precise, dual-axis, read the enemy before acting, never excessive force
- **MUST_NOT_COPY**: Dark elf appearance/race elements, Underdark setting, specific equipment names
- **USED_IN**: Pip (exile who makes the world worth living in)
- **AVAILABLE_IN**: A morally inverted character who made the same quality of choices in the other direction; a Resonance elder who chose knowledge over belonging

### GROMPH BAENRE
- **FRANCHISE**: Forgotten Realms
- **FUNCTION**: Shadow-intelligence, ruthless calculation behind cultivated surfaces
- **LIGHT_SIDE**: The ruthlessness is in service of something — it's not random cruelty; genuinely extraordinary capability
- **SHADOW_SIDE**: Will burn anything in the path to the goal; considers others' suffering as data
- **RELATIONSHIP_STYLE**: Transactional but not dishonest about being transactional
- **POWER_STYLE**: Arcane, precise, overwhelming when deployed, prefers not to be visible
- **MUST_NOT_COPY**: Drow appearance/race elements, specific Menzoberranzan politics
- **USED_IN**: Pip's shadow layer (the part that calculates ruthlessly, burns bridges to save what matters)
- **AVAILABLE_FOR**: A Resonance faction member who is effective and frightening; a villain who is entirely correct about the facts and entirely wrong about what to do with them

### ARES (God of War)
- **FRANCHISE**: Greek mythology / multiple adaptations
- **FUNCTION**: War-god energy, beauty as weapon, ancient grievance, presence that fills rooms
- **LIGHT_SIDE**: War as honest rather than political; the soldier who doesn't dress it up; capacity for real love under the violence
- **SHADOW_SIDE**: The grievance can outlast its cause; the love is territorial; the presence can crush what it's trying to protect
- **RELATIONSHIP_STYLE**: Possession followed by abandonment followed by fury at the abandonment
- **POWER_STYLE**: Overwhelming frontal force, makes you want to follow him and fight him simultaneously
- **MUST_NOT_COPY**: Specific Greek mythology elements, particular visual iconography
- **USED_IN**: Rylos (VERY Ares — this is his primary)
- **AVAILABLE_FOR**: Any major antagonist with real love under the violence; a faction leader whose methods are honest about being war

### PROFESSOR MCGONAGALL
- **FRANCHISE**: Harry Potter
- **FUNCTION**: Transmutational identity as self; institutional authority that chooses people over institutions when it matters
- **LIGHT_SIDE**: Precision, loyalty expressed through rigor, the teacher who prepared you for what you'd actually face
- **SHADOW_SIDE**: The precision can become rigidity; the institution she serves can be wrong; the armor doesn't always come off in time
- **RELATIONSHIP_STYLE**: High standards as a form of love; expects you to be capable of what you're capable of
- **POWER_STYLE**: Transformation — changing what things ARE rather than overpowering them
- **MUST_NOT_COPY**: Specific Hogwarts elements, animagus tabby cat form, HP-specific magic
- **USED_IN**: Rylos (transmutation is key to self-identity; changes what things ARE)
- **AVAILABLE_FOR**: An Iron Accord leader who uses administrative transmutation (redefining what resources are, what agreements mean); a morally-mixed character who changes what people are for good reasons that require terrible methods

### FREDDY KRUEGER
- **FRANCHISE**: A Nightmare on Elm Street
- **FUNCTION**: Invasive dreamlike threat, intimate psychological predation, taunting presence, violation of safe mental space
- **LIGHT_SIDE**: There isn't one in the source — which is WHY this is useful as a shadow-only input
- **SHADOW_SIDE**: The violation of what should be safe. The personalization of the threat. The intimacy of knowing what you fear before you do.
- **RELATIONSHIP_STYLE**: Predatory. Knows you before you know him. The familiarity is the weapon.
- **POWER_STYLE**: Dreams as penetration vectors; psychological architecture as the battlefield
- **MUST_NOT_COPY**: Burned appearance, glove weapon, Elm Street setting
- **USED_IN**: Xethrolund's shadow layer (invasive archive-reach into minds — TRANSFORMED into glyph/NSQ register)
- **AVAILABLE_FOR**: An antagonist who operates through information about you (what they know IS the weapon); a nightmare sequence source character

### JASON VOORHEES
- **FRANCHISE**: Friday the 13th
- **FUNCTION**: Relentless pursuit, cannot be stopped, arrives when things are worst, unstoppable survival-force
- **LIGHT_SIDE**: There isn't one in the source — which is WHY this is a shadow-only input for MACK

- **RELATIONSHIP_STYLE**: None. Presence. Motion. Arrival.
- **POWER_STYLE**: Physical, persistent, endures damage that should be terminal
- **MUST_NOT_COPY**: Specific slasher elements, camp lake setting, hockey mask
- **USED_IN**: Mack's shadow layer (grotesque form's relentless pursuit energy — TRANSFORMED into geological/cosmic register)
- **AVAILABLE_FOR**: A faction soldier who is still fighting a war that ended; a monster-type character who protects a location

- **USED_IN**: Rolzen (emotional palette — the profane protector who is secretly all tenderness)
