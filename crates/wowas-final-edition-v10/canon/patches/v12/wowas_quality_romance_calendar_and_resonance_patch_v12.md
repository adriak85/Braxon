# WoWaS Quality, Romance, Calendar, and Resonance Patch v12

## Purpose
This patch adds a broad quality-check layer for:
- beats
- characters
- creatures
- content
- action
- heated romantic scenes
- wholesome scenes
- birthdays and holiday moments
- resonance shaping for the central bond structure

Canon accuracy alone is not enough.
A scene can be canon-legal and still read weak, repetitive, tonally flat, socially thin, creature-wrong, action-blurry, romance-cold, or emotionally underfed.

This patch exists to stop that.

---

## Core law
Every meaningful beat, scene, character use, creature behavior, and action sequence should be checked for:
1. clarity
2. impact
3. memorability
4. lived feeling
5. public read
6. world-survival logic
7. emotional consequence
8. reread value

If something is technically valid but weak on multiple axes, it remains revision debt.

---

## Master quality-check categories

### 1. Beat quality check
Each beat should be reviewed for:
- purpose clarity
- emotional movement
- concrete event value
- setup / payoff integrity
- connector value to surrounding beats
- scene-worthiness
- whether it belongs on page instead of in outline only

Flag if a beat is:
- abstract but not lived
- repetitive of nearby beats
- emotionally flat
- more bookkeeping than story
- scaffold disguised as payoff
- exciting in concept but dull in scene terms

### 2. Character quality check
Each character should be checked for:
- distinct voice
- role clarity
- fascination, danger, warmth, or desirability as appropriate
- recurrence usefulness
- world fit
- whether the character changes scenes when present
- whether the character deserves to stay or should be merged / dropped

Flag if a character reads:
- generic
- redundant
- overderived without local truth
- underused despite high potential
- present but structurally irrelevant

### 3. Creature quality check
Each creature should be checked for:
- first reader read
- best public one-sentence description
- worst public one-sentence description
- survival logic
- world-fit
- action readability
- scene-use ceiling
- misread risk

Rule:
A creature may be horrifying, erotic, tender, majestic, lethal, uncanny, or beautiful, but its intended read must survive being described plainly.

### 4. Content and action quality check
Each action sequence should be checked for:
- clear spatial readability
- velocity
- danger
- distinctiveness
- body logic
- aftermath / cost
- emotional consequence
- witness value

Flag if action is:
- vague
- samey
- all spectacle / no consequence
- physically unreadable
- intense but not memorable

## Romantic and heated scene quality rule
Heated romantic scenes are part of quality review and should not be excluded.

They should be checked for:
- chemistry
- emotional specificity
- distinct body language
- whether the heat reveals character rather than merely occupying space
- whether the scene changes the relationship after it occurs
- whether it fits the arc stage
- whether it reads adult, intentional, and alive

Bad signs:
- generic heat
- copy-paste attraction language
- no emotional consequence
- body-only intensity without relational change
- overclinical wording
- overpurple wording

Good signs:
- specific longing
- specific vulnerability
- changed trust
- changed confidence
- changed fear
- changed bond law
- distinct voice even under desire

---

## Wholesome and recovery quality rule
Wholesome scenes should be checked just as hard as dramatic ones.

They should carry:
- warmth
- earned relief
- memory value
- domestic specificity
- humor or softness with real character weight
- evidence that the world is worth saving

These scenes should include, where appropriate:
- meals
- shared rest
- teasing
- dogs or animals
- birthdays
- ordinary joy
- awkward sweetness
- repair after strain
- changed comfort with each other

Rule:
A wholesome scene is not filler if it deepens attachment, proves safety, or lets cost land by contrast.

---

## Birthdays and holiday integration rule
Birthdays and holidays should be treated as structural opportunities, not decorative trivia.

Use them for:
- relationship contrast
- cultural texture
- ritual law
- emotional memory
- grief by comparison to lost normalcy
- proof that people still celebrate in a wounded world
- fragile joy after devastation

Each major birthday or holiday scene should be checked for:
- ritual specificity
- food / object / music / custom detail
- who remembers and who forgets
- who feels joy, grief, longing, shame, envy, or comfort
- whether the occasion changes the scene beyond calendar mention

## Rylos directive
Rylos should read as apex Aries-coded.

That means his scene-quality and character-quality pass should emphasize:
- forceful forward identity
- pride
- directness under pressure
- sharpened courage
- reactive fire
- competitive charge
- refusal to yield emotionally or strategically unless deeply moved
- charisma that can read dangerous, hot, funny, and maddening in the same scene

Rule:
Do not flatten him into generic anger. He should feel like peak living fire with wit, edge, ego, and actual heart.

Xethrolund should complement Pip through:
