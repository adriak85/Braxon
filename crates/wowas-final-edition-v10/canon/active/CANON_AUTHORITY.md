# WoWaS Canon Authority

For the reconstruction branch, the active 33-book serial is authoritative. Legacy and superseded narrative material remains source/audit material unless explicitly restored by an active canonical record.

The active serial order is `book_spine_33.tsv`. The canonical scene inventory consumed by the story-seed/runtime path is `../wowas_clean_scene_index_v2.tsv`.

Character continuity is governed by `character_timeline_lattice_v14_33.tsv`; inclusion and exclusion are governed by `canon_laws.tsv` and `canon_blocklist.tsv`.

Do not treat deprecated Fifteen-year-old, Chrono, Aortic-Labyrinth, or other blocklisted material as active merely because historical copies remain in the repository.
