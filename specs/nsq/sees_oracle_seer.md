# NSQ Notice Triad

## SEES ALL
Total notice.
Warns on anomaly onset, horizon pressure, and broad drift.

## Seer
Faint perception.
Detects weak-signal meaning before ordinary proof is assembled.

## Oracle
Consequential assertion.
Frames probable lawful outcomes from currently known structure.

## Constraint
- SEES ALL warns
- Seer hints
- Oracle asserts
- none may alone convict, punish, or overrule the court
