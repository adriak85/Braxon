## Required final-book behavior

A resurrection is not complete merely because a character returns. It must carry a consequence comparable to the death: changed relationships, altered world state, changed self-understanding, and a distinct response from multiple main-party viewpoints. The audit records evidence counts and leaves any insufficiently evidenced character blocked from prose promotion.
