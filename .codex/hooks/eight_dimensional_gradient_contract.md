The eight dimensional gradient exists to:
- preserve semantic continuity
- preserve operational causality
- preserve emotional semantic weighting
- preserve multidirectional resolution
- preserve reconstruction continuity
- preserve non-flat semantic traversal

The gradient is:
- operational
- semantic
- causal
- multidimensional
- execution relevant

- decorative metadata
- static categorization

- simple nearest-neighbor lookup
- isolated vector storage
